## Adopted direction and authority boundary

Human selected for **NarrativeLine** the distinction already adopted locally for [LiaisonScape](../../e2r-spec/docs/liaisonscape/liaisonscape-dimension-assessment-recognition-audit-2026-10-24.md#subsequent-human-design-direction--2026-10-24-jst): know an Extension's exact identifier and version without necessarily providing its display or editing features. Functional non-support alone need not create an ordinary fault notice. An actual save, Export, validation, compatibility or preservation effect must still be explained, while legitimate technical Diagnostics remain exact and accessible. This is not a new E2R-wide UI standard or a Specification change. The [Specification Extension](../../e2r-spec/extensions/specification-extension.md) declares used versions, not application capabilities. Dimension Assessment [0.2.0](../../e2r-spec/extensions/dimension-assessment-extension-candidate-0.2.0.md) remains a non-Stable Candidate; exact 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 contracts must not be conflated.

LiaisonScape has the same recognition-versus-feature principle and has separately chosen in-context expandable technical detail for L3, while retaining published Validator 0.7.0 and its Lighthouse warnings. NarrativeLine already has its own [accepted Diagnostics Presentation](public-beta-f2-and-diagnostics-presentation-2026-10-27.md#diagnostics-presentation-formal-human-acceptance--2026-10-27), with Current/Candidate ownership and expandable Code/Severity/Path. Neither LiaisonScape's UI layout nor its bounded Lighthouse preservation acceptance transfers to NarrativeLine. NarrativeLine's future notification conditions must fit its Timeline, replacement dialog, Import, Resume and Export paths.

## Current implementation and first case

NarrativeLine pins and installs published `@sukoyaka-dopeness/e2r-validator@0.7.0`. For bundled Cedar Observatory EN/JA, current `tests/CedarObservatoryShowcase.test.js` confirms valid Import, **two warning Diagnostics** (`unknown_extension`, `specification_unavailable`), valid Export and re-Import, and equality of the imported and re-imported Dataset object. The payload's exact identifier is `draft.github.sukoyaka-dopeness.dimension-assessment`, its `specVersion` and Specification `uses` version are `0.2.0`, and this fixture contains four Dimensions and 15 Assessments. These are a fixture/service round trip, not all-edit preservation or a fresh Human Browser check. The same two warnings remain the **accepted 0.2.3 behavior** and must not be hidden in this release.

The installed Validator 0.7.0 does not register that identifier or support its exact Specification declaration. It therefore emits `unknown_extension` for the payload and `specification_unavailable` for the declared use. The adjacent `e2r-validator` checkout contains later exact-version recognition and Dimension Assessment semantic validation while still declaring package version 0.7.0; it is **not** NarrativeLine's installed published dependency. Under a future released Validator, malformed known 0.2.0 data may become a real error rather than the present warning-only accepted import. A local parser may read the identifier and `specVersion`, but that observation must not be reported as Validator recognition or semantic validation.

Current `ImportWarningPresentation` groups these codes with Extension-recognition notices. Its ordinary EN/JA text says this app cannot fully inspect the information and cannot guarantee survival through every edit or Export; exact Validator results remain in expandable details for Current and Candidate. Thus the current wording is cautious and technically attributable, but it does not express the desired distinction between a *known exact Candidate with no NarrativeLine feature UI* and a genuinely unknown identifier or unsupported version. Merely rewording or filtering those two codes as harmless would be incorrect while Validator 0.7.0 still reports a recognition limit.

## Future decision and acceptance gate — at the 2026-10-27 assessment checkpoint

Before implementation, Human must choose a compatibility route: **(A)** an application-local exact-ID/version informational recognition layer while retaining Validator 0.7.0 and its legitimate technical warnings, or **(B)** a separately released and reviewed Validator with exact Candidate recognition and semantic validation. Route A cannot claim that the Validator checked payload correctness or erase its warnings; route B requires Validator release authorization, package integration review, and explicit assessment of newly rejected datasets and safe recovery. This document does not choose a route or define how an ordinary notice is classified for every mixed/error case.

For either route, decide the precise Current/Candidate/Operation UI conditions and EN/JA copy, how technical diagnostics remain discoverable, when a preservation limit warrants a notice, and what to do when declarations are absent or conflict. Test known-valid 0.2.0, malformed known 0.2.0, 0.1.0, future version, truly unknown Extension, mixed warnings and real errors; check Import/Handoff refusal, Cancel/Accept, save/Export refusal, unknown payload retention, keyboard access and representative browser flows. Technical Code/Severity/Path and Dataset ownership must remain exact. Any newly required Validator, Runtime, schema or contract change returns to Human before execution.
